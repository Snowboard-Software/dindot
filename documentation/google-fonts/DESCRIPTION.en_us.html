DINDOT is a rounded industrial sans serif derived from DINish. Its four weights have matching italics, circular dots, a dotted zero, tabular figures and stylistic alternatives for numeric text. The family includes Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, Vietnamese and mathematical characters.

The GetDot modifications build on the work of the DINish Project Authors. DINish derives from Altinn-DIN and D-DIN; the original D-DIN design is by Charles Nix / Monotype, commissioned by Datto.

Explore the interactive specimen at the DINDOT project homepage. To contribute or report an issue, visit github.com/Snowboard-Software/dindot.
